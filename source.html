FAQs

What is Source by Webflow?

Source by Webflow is a new platform for marketing teams and their agents to launch, optimize, and scale digital experiences, together. Source connects with your codebase and martech stack, bringing agentic and visual development into a shared operating environment, with every marketer, designer, and developer getting a workspace tailored to their job and the outcomes they own.

What happens to the Webflow platform I've already built my site on?

Webflow powers digital experiences for hundreds of thousands of organizations around the world, and the platform you know and love isn’t going anywhere. As we build Source, we will continue to invest in our existing platform that teams use every day, including expanding its agentic capabilities. You've seen this in our recent launches, from MCP 2.0 and 2.1 to native AEO agents to AI Code Components, with much more on the roadmap that we shared in our Webflow Conference keynote. When Source is ready for you, and if you choose to migrate to our new platform, we will provide a supported path.

How does code as the foundation unlock more agent capability?

Code is the native language of AI. When agents can work directly with the code that actually ships (instead of an abstraction layer) they can understand your whole system directly, make changes to it, and evaluate their own work without limitations (unless you set them). That’s a fundamental shift from how Webflow has worked historically, and what lets Source unlock agents as top-tier teammates, not just tools you prompt. For more context behind our decision to make code the foundation of Source, check out this blog post. Can Source work with the stack I already have?
